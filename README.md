# 🥤 Coca-Cola Sales Analysis Using Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing Coca-Cola sales performance across major retailers and regions in the United States using Microsoft Excel. The objective is to identify sales trends, evaluate retailer performance, understand pricing strategies, and assess operating profitability through data-driven insights.

The project uses Excel data analysis techniques, PivotTables, charts, and interactive dashboards to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyse total sales and units sold across different retailers.
- Compare sales performance across geographical regions.
- Evaluate the relationship between unit price, sales volume, and profitability.
- Identify top-performing and underperforming retailers.
- Examine monthly sales trends and seasonal patterns.
- Identify opportunities to improve revenue and operating margins.

## 📂 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation
- Imported and reviewed multi-sheet Excel datasets.
- Standardised column names and cleaned inconsistent headers.
- Handled missing values and incorrect data entries.
- Converted date and numerical columns into appropriate formats.
- Structured important sales and profitability metrics.

### 2. Sales & Profitability Analysis
- Analysed total revenue and units sold.
- Compared retailer-wise and regional sales performance.
- Evaluated price per unit and its impact on sales.
- Identified profitable and underperforming markets.
- Examined operating margin variations.

### 3. Time-Based Analysis
- Conducted monthly sales analysis.
- Identified seasonal sales trends.
- Evaluated peak and low-performing sales periods.
- Monitored changes in profitability over time.

### 4. Data Visualization & Dashboard
Developed visual representations using:
- Bar Charts – Retailer-wise sales comparison.
- Line Charts – Monthly sales trends.
- Heatmaps – Regional sales performance.
- PivotTables – Dynamic data summarisation.
- Slicers – Interactive filtering and exploration.
